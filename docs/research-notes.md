# UX Research & Discovery Notes

## 1. Project Background
- **Program:** SkillNexus UI/UX Internship
- **Sprint:** Week 3 Deliverables
- **Focus Area:** User Research, Pain Point Discovery, and Information Architecture

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Many digital platforms suffer from steep learning curves, cluttered dashboards, and buried core actions. Users often abandon key flows mid-way due to lack of immediate feedback and confusing navigation patterns.

---

## 3. Target Audience & Personas
- **Primary Users:** Students, early-career professionals, and everyday digital users looking for streamlined productivity.
- **Key Characteristics:**
  - Fast-paced workflow with low tolerance for cognitive overload.
  - Expects mobile-first, clean, and intuitive touch targets.
  - Relies heavily on visual hierarchy rather than dense textual instructions.

---

## 4. Key Pain Points Identified
1. **Navigation Clutter:** Too many top-level options causing decision fatigue.
2. **Hidden CTAs:** Primary actions not visually distinct from secondary links.
3. **Missing System Status:** Unclear error states and missing progress bars during multistep flows.

---

## 5. Research Conclusions & Strategic Direction
- Limit bottom/top navigation to a maximum of 4–5 core items.
- Introduce persistent floating action buttons (FAB) or high-contrast sticky primary CTAs.
- Break down complex multi-screen actions into step-by-step progressive disclosure cards.
